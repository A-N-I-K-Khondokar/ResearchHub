# CSE Research Hub 🎓

**CSE Research Hub** is a mobile application built for students, faculty, and researchers in the Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) department. 

It is a **people-first research discovery and networking platform** designed to answer one central question:

> *"Who is researching what in our CSE community right now?"*

---

## 🌟 What is CSE Research Hub?

Academic research often remains scattered across individual personal websites, buried in PDF archives, or locked behind formal citation databases. As a student or faculty member, finding out who in your own department is working on topics like **Bangla NLP**, **Computer Vision**, or **Distributed Systems** is difficult.

CSE Research Hub bridges that gap through a simple three-step journey:

$$\textbf{DISCOVER} \longrightarrow \textbf{UNDERSTAND} \longrightarrow \textbf{CONNECT}$$

### 💡 Core Features & Purpose

* 🔍 **Discover Ongoing Research:** Find out what your peers, seniors, and professors are actively working on before it even gets published.
* 🧠 **Explore by Topic:** Click on any research domain (e.g., *Machine Learning*, *Cybersecurity*, *HCI*) to see all the researchers in the department who share that interest.
* 🚀 **Share Current Work in Progress:** Post your thesis topic, semester research project, or paper draft so potential collaborators can find you.
* 📚 **Showcase Completed Publications:** Keep your academic portfolio up-to-date with direct links to your papers on Google Scholar, ResearchGate, IEEE Xplore, or GitHub.
* 🤝 **Academic Networking:** Send connection requests and collaborate with fellow researchers across different batches and research labs.

---

## 🚫 What This Application is NOT

To keep the platform focused and high-signal, CSE Research Hub is **NOT**:
* ❌ A paper writing or LaTeX drafting tool.
* ❌ A PDF storage or file hosting repository.
* ❌ A replacement for Google Scholar or ResearchGate (it links directly to them).
* ❌ A social media clone (no vanity follower counts, likes, or algorithmic feeds).
* ❌ A project management system.

---

## 📱 How to Use the App

### 1. Create Your Research Identity
* Sign up with your departmental email.
* Select your primary research interests (e.g., *Natural Language Processing*, *Bioinformatics*, *Robotics*).
* Link your existing academic profiles (Google Scholar, GitHub, ResearchGate, or personal website).

### 2. Discover What's Happening (Home Feed)
* Browse the latest updates from members of the CSE community.
* See active research momentum—who is collecting datasets, formulating hypotheses, or drafting papers.

### 3. Explore & Find Collaborators ("Who's Working On This?")
* Search for specific keywords or explore topics via chips.
* View researcher profiles, read their bios, and see what they are currently investigating.

### 4. Share Your Work
* Tap the **Share** button to post a research update.
* Give it a title, a short summary, tag relevant topics, and indicate your current stage (*Ideation*, *Experimentation*, *Drafting*, or *Pre-print*).

### 5. Connect & Collaborate
* Send a connection request to professors or peers.
* Receive notifications when someone wants to collaborate or mentions your research area.

## 🚀 How to Run the App Locally

### Prerequisites
Make sure you have the following installed on your computer:
* [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (version 18 or newer)
* [Expo Go](https://expo.dev/go) app installed on your physical Android or iOS device (available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store).

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Clone or open the repository:**
   ```bash
   cd ResearchHub
   ```

2. **Install project dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the development server:**
   ```bash
   npx expo start
   ```

4. **Open the app on your device:**
   * **Android Device:** Open the **Expo Go** app and scan the QR code displayed in your terminal.
   * **iOS Device:** Open the default **Camera** app, scan the QR code, and tap the link to open in Expo Go.
   * **Web Preview:** Press `w` in your terminal to view in your web browser.
